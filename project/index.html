Project

Peak deconvolution of voltammetry signals

An outline of what Team 1 is building and how the work is divided. The week-by-week plan, with progress, is on the dashboard.

The problem

In square-wave voltammetry, a sample containing several analytes produces a current response in which the individual peaks overlap. Separating that combined response back into per-analyte peaks is what the project calls deconvolution.

The approach

Team 1 is the deep-learning track. The plan runs from acquiring local measurements and preparing existing data, through model development and adaptation, to explainability work and a web interface that serves the pipeline.

No results yet. This page describes the plan. Accuracy figures, comparisons and conclusions will only appear once the corresponding work packages have actually been run.

Scope

What is and is not in the plan

Planned

  • Local dataset acquisition alongside preparation of an existing dataset.
  • Detection and quantification models, with quantification approached both as classification and as regression.
  • Adaptation and retraining of those pipelines on the locally acquired data.
  • Explainability work, including faithfulness and stability testing of the explanations.
  • A web interface with data upload, result visualisation and end-to-end documentation.

Bounded on purpose

  • The chatbot is a single subtask, WP6.3, limited to queries about results.
  • Fixed delivery weeks in the schedule cannot be moved; the earlier weeks of a task can be rearranged around them.
  • This site covers Team 1's deep-learning track only.
