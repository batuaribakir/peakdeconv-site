Focus

What the project is about

A short orientation. The full outline lives on the Project page, and the week-by-week plan on the Dashboard.

The measurement

Square-wave voltammetry on a sample that holds more than one analyte produces peaks that sit on top of one another. Reading a single analyte off that combined curve is the difficulty the project starts from.

The track

Team 1 works the deep-learning route: acquire and prepare data, develop and adapt models, make their behaviour explainable, and serve the whole pipeline through a web interface.

The plan

Six work packages across fifteen weeks, 23 subtasks in total, with a midterm in week 8. The schedule is generated from the project workbook rather than written by hand.
